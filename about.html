The Leadership Journey

Rooted in Seva, Driven by Professional Excellence

Rajesh Gurusamy brings together an exceptional combination of a quarter-century political organisation track record and modern corporate executive management.

Beginning in 2001 as a grassroots youth leader in Watrap, Virudhunagar district, he rose systematically through Union, District, and State leadership tiers in the Bharatiya Janata Party. Concurrently, since establishing his base in Chennai in 2005, he has founded and directed successful corporate ventures in green facility engineering, renewable solar EPC, EV charging systems, and agricultural supply manufacturing.

State Vice President — Kisan Morcha, BJP Tamil Nadu
  • Farmer Welfare: Spearheaded outreach for central schemes including PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi, Kisan Credit Cards, and promotion of Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs).
  • Cadre Building: 25+ years continuous organisational discipline from Watrap Union Yuva Morcha (2001) to State Commerce Cell Secretary (2014) to Kisan Morcha State Vice President (2025).
  • Strategic Vision: Committed to party modernization, booth-level digitisation, and long-term socio-political development towards the 2031 Tamil Nadu Assembly.
Chairman & Managing Director — Guru Group of Enterprises
  • Guru Integrated Services India Pvt. Ltd.: Leading facility management, green landscaping, Solar EPC power installations, and EV charging station setups.
  • Guru Properties & Financial Services: Strategic commercial asset guidance, real estate advisory, and corporate structuring.
  • Rajesh Agro Industries: Direct agricultural tools manufacturing, farm implement wholesale, and sustainable soil inputs.
  • Nupuram Impex India Pvt. Ltd.: International export-import trade management adhering to global standards.
